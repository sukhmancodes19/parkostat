ParkSense

AI-powered smart parking detection — wireless sensors, live occupancy, predictive analytics
Business Model Canvas
The problem
Drivers waste time hunting for parking in malls, hospitals, airports, universities and office garages — causing congestion, fuel waste, and lost revenue for property owners.
Why incumbents fail
Bosch, Siemens, ParkHelp and SKIDATA charge $250–600 per space with wired installs, proprietary lock-in, and poor scalability.
Our wedge
Wireless sensors + AI detection at a target ₹700–1,500 per space — installed in minutes, priced for mid-market deployment.
